Booking

A booking is confirmed only when we send written confirmation, after you accept a quote and any required deposit has been received. Quotes show the date, times, room, guest count, inclusions, taxes and the terms that apply.

Setup and cleanup time is included in your reserved hours. Guest numbers must stay within the approved limit for your room and layout.

Changes

You can request a change to your date, times, guest numbers or package. Your existing booking stays in place until the change has been checked for availability, priced and accepted. Any price difference is shown in a new quote.

Privacy

We use the details you give us to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes and manage your booking. We do not sell your personal information. We send marketing messages only if you opt in, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

Your information is stored securely and kept only as long as needed for your booking and for legal obligations. To ask about your data, email hello@eventhall.example.

Accessibility statement

We aim to make this website usable by everyone, following WCAG 2.2 AA guidance. It supports keyboard navigation, screen readers, zoom and reduced-motion settings.

If you find a barrier or need information in another format, please contact us. We will respond and confirm what we can offer for your visit.
