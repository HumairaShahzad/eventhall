Booking

Is sending a request the same as booking?

No. A request lets us check availability. Your date is reserved only after you accept a written quote and any required deposit is received.

How long does it take to hear back?

We aim to reply as soon as possible. You receive a reference number straight away when you send a request.

Can you hold a date while I decide?

Yes, when a quote is sent. The quote shows how long the date is held. Holds are not guaranteed after they expire.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Deposit requirements are shown in your quote, along with the due date for any remaining balance.

Can I change my date or guest numbers later?

Yes, subject to availability and the terms of your booking. Changes are priced and confirmed before your original booking is altered. See the changes policy.

Catering

Do you offer food and drink?

Yes. We offer menus for every package, and tastings can be arranged on request. Catering is quoted separately.

Can we bring our own caterer?

This depends on the room and the event. Tell us your plans when you enquire so we can confirm what is permitted.

Alcohol

Can we serve alcohol?

Alcohol can be served under our licensing arrangements. We confirm the options and any restrictions in your quote.

Who needs a licence?

Licensing rules depend on your location and the type of event. We will explain what applies to your booking.

Setup and cleanup

How much time do we get?

Setup and cleanup time is included in your booked hours and shown on your quote and confirmation.

Can we arrive early to decorate?

Early access can usually be arranged for an additional fee. Ask when you make your enquiry.

Noise

Is there a music curfew?

Yes. Amplified music must finish by the time agreed in your booking, to respect neighbours. Details are in the booking terms.

Accessibility

Is the venue accessible?

We want every guest to feel welcome. Tell us about access needs and we will confirm routes, facilities and seating for your date. See our accessibility statement.

Is there accessible parking?

Please see the contact page for parking details, or ask us before your event.
