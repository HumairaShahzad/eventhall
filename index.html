How it works

From first question to confirmed date.

  1. Step 1

    Check your date

    Choose your event type, date, times and guest count.

  2. Step 2

    Send your request

    We check the room and schedule for your date.

  3. Step 3

    Receive your quote

    You get an itemised quote, or an invitation to tour first.

  4. Step 4

    Confirm

    Accept the quote and terms. Your date is reserved once confirmed.

A request is not a booking. A date is only reserved after you accept a quote and any required deposit is paid.
